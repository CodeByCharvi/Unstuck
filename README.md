# Unstuck
**you're not lazy. you're just stuck.**

that feeling when you have 47 things to do and you're lying there watching reels about productivity instead of doing anything? yeah. this fixes that.

one button. one task. no excuses.

---

## what it does

drop everything that's living rent free in your head into the app. hit the button. it picks one thing and tells you to go do it. that's literally it.

no ai subscription. no 5am routine. no notion template. just you and one task.

---

## 🔥 features

- **one task at a time** — because your brain can't handle more rn
- **smart images** — type "gym" and it knows. type "swimming" and it knows. it just knows.
- **dark / light mode** — we don't judge your aesthetic
- **bored section** — 20 actually cool things to do instead of doom scrolling (skateboarding, pottery, freediving, film photography and more)
- **no account. no login. no bs** — your tasks live in your browser, private and offline
- **parallax + animations** — it's giving main character energy
- **zero dependencies** — one html file. that's the whole app.
